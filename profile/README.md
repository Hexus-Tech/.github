## About Hexus Tech

Hexus Tech brings together educational material, technical experimentation, and software projects across programming, data analysis, quantitative research, and emerging technologies.

This profile highlights **publicly accessible repositories**. Other initiatives may remain private or be in early development; their absence from this page does not imply they have been discontinued.

## Learning resources

### Hexus Tech Educational Library

An open collection of Markdown articles and educational resources for self-directed learning in artificial intelligence, data science, programming, and emerging technologies.

[![Explore Library](https://img.shields.io/badge/Explore-Educational%20Library-3FB950?style=flat-square)](https://github.com/Hexus-Tech/hexus-library)

EdBooks, notebooks, and other long-form learning materials are part of the broader educational direction. Individual repositories are linked here only when they are publicly accessible.
